How to read a garment HSN code

Take 62052090 as an example. 62 is the chapter (woven apparel). 6205 is the heading (men's or boys' shirts). 620520 is the internationally shared subheading (of cotton). The last two digits, 90, are India's national split.

Because the first six digits are shared by most customs systems worldwide, they give your broker a reliable starting point. The final import code and duty rate come from your own country's tariff.

Questions buyers ask

What is an HSN code for garments?

HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) codes classify goods for customs and tax. Garments fall in chapter 61 if knitted and chapter 62 if woven. India uses 8 digit codes; the first 6 digits are shared internationally.

What is the difference between chapter 61 and chapter 62?

Chapter 61 covers knitted or crocheted garments such as T shirts and leggings. Chapter 62 covers woven garments such as shirts, trousers, jeans, dresses and blazers.

What is the HSN code for cotton T shirts?

Cotton T shirts are invoiced under 61091000 in our catalogue, for men, women and kids.

What HSN code applies to babies' garments?

Babies' garments use heading 6209: 62092000 for cotton, 62093000 for synthetic fibres and 62099090 for other textiles. Babies' garments are for children of a body height not exceeding 86 cm.
