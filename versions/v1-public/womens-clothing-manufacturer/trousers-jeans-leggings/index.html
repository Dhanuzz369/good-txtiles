About this range

Woven women's trousers, jeans and shorts are classified under HSN 6204.6x: cotton under 62046290 and synthetic fibre under 62046300. Leggings are knitted, so they move to chapter 61 under 61046200.

For leggings, stretch recovery and opacity are what buyers test first. We knit cotton with elastane at the GSM you set and run a squat test on the sample before bulk.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

Why are leggings a different HSN chapter from trousers?

Leggings are knitted, so they fall under chapter 61 (knitted garments) at 61046200, while woven trousers, jeans and shorts fall under chapter 62.

Can you make plus size ranges?

Yes. Send your plus size measurement chart and we grade the pattern for it rather than scaling up the standard sizes.
