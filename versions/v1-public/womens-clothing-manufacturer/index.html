FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

Can you print my own designs on blouses?

Yes. Send print files with colour references. We produce strike offs for approval, then a sample garment, before bulk printing.

Can a dress style be lined?

Yes. Dresses in sheer fabrics such as voile or georgette are usually half or fully lined. We include lining in the costing once you confirm it.

Why are leggings a different HSN chapter from trousers?

Leggings are knitted, so they fall under chapter 61 (knitted garments) at 61046200, while woven trousers, jeans and shorts fall under chapter 62.

Can kurta sets be supplied without the dupatta?

Yes. Two piece and three piece sets are both available. Tell us the components in your enquiry.

Can you match a blazer fit from another brand?

Send the reference garment and your size chart. We take measurements from it, adjust to your chart and fit the sample on a form before bulk.

Ready to source?

Send your spec or ask what is in stock. We reply with availability or a production costing.
