About this range

Babies' garments have their own HSN heading, 6209, defined in the Harmonized System as garments for young children of a body height not exceeding 86 cm. Cotton babywear is invoiced under 62092000, synthetic fibres under 62093000 and other textile materials under 62099090.

Babywear buyers ask first about softness, snaps and chemical safety. We use nickel free snaps on request, soft binding at necklines and can arrange third party testing to your market's requirements.

Anything else? Send us your question.

What counts as a babies' garment for customs?

Under the Harmonized System, babies' garments are for young children of a body height not exceeding 86 cm. Larger sizes are classified as children's garments under the regular men's, boys', women's or girls' headings.

Can you arrange product safety testing for babywear?

Yes. Tell us the destination market and the tests your buyer requires and we arrange testing with a third party laboratory before shipment.
