Children's clothing carries extra compliance work in most markets, from tracking labels to chemical limits. Tell us the destination market in your enquiry and we plan labelling and testing around it from the start.

Customs treats babies' garments as their own heading. Under the Harmonized System notes, they are garments for young children of a body height not exceeding 86 cm, which is why baby lines below use HSN 6209 while larger kids' sizes use the regular headings.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

What counts as a babies' garment for customs?

Under the Harmonized System, babies' garments are for young children of a body height not exceeding 86 cm. Larger sizes are classified as children's garments under the regular men's, boys', women's or girls' headings.

What age sizes do you produce for kids T shirts?

Typically 2 to 14 years, but we grade to your own size chart if your market uses height based or other sizing.

Why does kids shorts show two HSN codes?

Boys' shorts are classified with men's and boys' trousers (62034290) and girls' shorts with women's and girls' trousers (62046290). The invoice lists each by style.
