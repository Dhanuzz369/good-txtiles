Export market

Garment exporter from India to the UK

GoodBiz Apparels supplies UK importers, brands and retailers with garments made in India, from ready stock or to the buyer's spec, labelled for the UK market.

Send your enquiry See products and HSN codes

Labelling

The Textile Products (Labelling and Fibre Composition) Regulations 2012 require accurate fibre composition labels. Care symbols are not legally required in the UK but most retailers expect them. We print labels to your artwork.

Trade agreement

India and the UK signed a Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement in 2025 that includes textiles and garments. Whether preferential rates apply depends on its entry into force and rules of origin at your shipment date, so confirm with your broker.

Tariff

UK duty is set by the UK Global Tariff commodity code. We give the Indian HSN code for each line to help your broker classify.

This is general guidance, not legal or customs advice. Rules change; confirm current requirements with your customs broker.

Can you ship garments from India to the UK?

Yes. We ship ready stock and made to order garments with the commercial invoice, packing list and HSN codes your broker needs.

Who confirms the import duty?

Your customs broker classifies the goods under your country's tariff. We provide the Indian 8 digit HSN code for each line as a starting point.
