Export markets

Where we export garments from India

GoodBiz Apparels ships ready stock and made to order garments from India to importers in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, UAE and GCC, and Australia. Each market has its own labelling rules and trade terms.

United StatesGoodBiz Apparels supplies US importers, brands and retailers with garments made in India, from ready stock or produced to the buyer's spec, labelled and documented for US entry.Labelling, tariffs and documents United KingdomGoodBiz Apparels supplies UK importers, brands and retailers with garments made in India, from ready stock or to the buyer's spec, labelled for the UK market.Labelling, tariffs and documents European UnionGoodBiz Apparels supplies importers and brands across the European Union with garments made in India, from ready stock or produced to the buyer's spec.Labelling, tariffs and documents UAE and GCCGoodBiz Apparels supplies importers, wholesalers and retailers in the UAE and wider GCC with garments made in India, including western wear and Indian ethnic lines such as kurta sets.Labelling, tariffs and documents AustraliaGoodBiz Apparels supplies Australian importers, brands and retailers with garments made in India, from ready stock or produced to the buyer's spec.Labelling, tariffs and documents
