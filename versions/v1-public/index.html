What you can specify

Every detail that decides
how the garment sells.

Give us as much or as little as you have. If you only have a reference garment, we work out the spec with you and confirm it in writing before the sample. Read our GSM guide.

  • Fabric and GSM

    Cotton, polyester, silk, blends and handloom, weighed to the GSM you set.

  • Size chart and fit

    Graded to your measurements for your market, from baby sizes up.

  • Colours and prints

    Matched to your Pantone references or a physical swatch.

  • Labels and trims

    Your brand labels, care labels, hang tags, buttons and zips.

  • Packing

    Polybag, folding, ratio packing per carton and carton markings.

  • Export documents

    Invoice and packing list with the HSN code for each line.

How an order runs

From enquiry to your port
in three steps.

  1. 01

    Send the enquiry

    Products, quantities, spec or tech pack, and destination. We reply with stock availability or a costing for production.

  2. 02

    Approve stock photos or the sample

    For stock you see the actual lot. For custom orders you approve a physical sample before bulk production starts.

  3. 03

    Production, packing and shipment

    Goods are checked, packed to your carton plan and shipped with the invoice, packing list and HSN codes in order.

FAQ

Before you send
the enquiry.

Anything else? Send us your question.

Can I mix ready stock and made to order items in one shipment?

Yes. Many buyers take some lines from ready stock and have others manufactured to their spec. We consolidate both into one shipment where the timelines allow.

What do I need to send for a made to order enquiry?

A design or tech pack, your size chart, fabric and GSM, colours, quantity per size and colour, labelling and packing needs, and the destination port. A reference garment works if you have no tech pack.

What is the minimum order quantity?

{{MOQ per style}} for made to order styles. Ready stock depends on the lot, so tell us the quantity you need and we confirm what is available.

Do you make a sample before bulk production?

Yes. For custom orders we make a sample to your spec and start bulk production only after you approve it in writing.

Can you put our brand labels and tags on the garments?

Yes, on both made to order and ready stock lines. Send your label artwork or the physical labels and we attach them before packing.

Are the HSN codes in the catalogue final for customs?

They are the 8 digit codes we use on our export invoices for each product. Your import tariff code can differ by country, so your customs broker confirms the code on your side.
