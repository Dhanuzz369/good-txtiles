Ready stock

Ready stock garments for export from India

Ready stock garments are finished pieces already made and packed, so they can ship without waiting for a production run. GoodBiz Apparels holds ready stock across men's, women's and kids' lines and ships it to importers with full export documents.

Send your enquiry See products and HSN codes

How buying ready stock works

  1. Tell us what you need. Products, sizes, colours and quantities, plus your destination port.
  2. We confirm availability. You get the available quantities by size and colour with photos of the actual lot.
  3. Labels and packing. Add your own brand labels and hang tags if you want them, and confirm the carton plan.
  4. Proforma, payment and dispatch. We ship with the commercial invoice, packing list and HSN codes.

When ready stock is the right choice

  • You need goods for a season that starts soon.
  • You want to test a product in your market before committing to a production order.
  • You are replenishing a line that sold through.

Mixing stock with made to order

You can combine ready stock lines with made to order styles in one shipment. The stock lines wait until the production lines are ready, or ship ahead if you prefer.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

What is ready stock in garments?

Ready stock garments are finished, packed pieces already produced, so they can ship without a new production run.

Can I see the stock before paying?

Yes. We send photos of the actual lot, with quantities by size and colour, before you pay.

Can you add my brand labels to ready stock?

Yes. Send your labels or artwork and we attach them before packing.

Ready to source?

Send your spec or ask what is in stock. We reply with availability or a production costing.
