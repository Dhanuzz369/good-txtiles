Garment sourcing guides for importers

Plain answers to the questions buyers ask before placing a garment order with an Indian manufacturer: fabric weight, tech packs, import steps and customs codes.

Fabric GSM guide for garment buyersGSM means grams per square metre. It is the weight of one square metre of fabric, and it is the standard way buyers and mills agree how heavy a garment fabric should be. A higher GSM means a heavier, usually thicker fabric.Read the guide How to send a tech pack to a garment manufacturerA tech pack is the instruction file a factory uses to make your garment exactly as you intend. A complete tech pack gets you an accurate costing, a right first time sample and fewer surprises in bulk.Read the guide How to import garments from IndiaImporting garments from India follows six steps: enquiry, costing, sample approval, production, inspection and shipment with export documents. Ready stock orders skip the sample and production steps and can ship sooner.Read the guide HSN codes for garmentsThe 8 digit codes for 38 garment lines, and how to read them.Read the guide
