Guide

How to import garments from India

Importing garments from India follows six steps: enquiry, costing, sample approval, production, inspection and shipment with export documents. Ready stock orders skip the sample and production steps and can ship sooner.

Updated 2026-11-04 by the GoodBiz Apparels sourcing team

Step by step

  1. Enquiry. Share products, quantities, spec or tech pack, and destination port.
  2. Costing and proforma invoice. The supplier confirms price, Incoterm, payment terms and lead time.
  3. Sample approval. For made to order, approve a physical sample. For ready stock, approve photos of the actual lot.
  4. Production. Fabric is sourced to your GSM, then cut, sewn, finished and packed.
  5. Inspection. An internal or third party final inspection checks quantity, measurements and workmanship.
  6. Shipment. Goods ship by sea or air with export documents.

Export documents you receive

DocumentPurpose
Commercial invoiceValue, HSN code per line and terms of sale
Packing listCartons, sizes, quantities, weights and dimensions
Bill of lading or air waybillProof of shipment and title to the goods
Certificate of originNeeded to claim preferential duty under a trade agreement

Common Incoterms

  • EXW: you collect from the factory and handle everything after.
  • FOB: the supplier clears export and loads the goods at the Indian port; you pay freight onward.
  • CIF: the supplier also pays sea freight and insurance to your port.
  • DDP: the supplier delivers duty paid to your door. Less common for garments.

HSN codes and your import tariff

Indian exporters declare goods with an 8 digit HSN code. The first 6 digits are shared internationally under the Harmonized System, so your broker can use them as a starting point. The final import code and duty rate are set by your country's tariff. See our HSN code list for garments.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

How long does it take to import garments from India?

Ready stock can ship as soon as payment and documents are in order. Made to order needs time for sampling and production plus sea or air transit. Your supplier gives the lead time in the proforma invoice.

Which Incoterm is best for a first order?

FOB is common because the supplier handles Indian export clearance and you choose your own freight forwarder.
