FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

What HSN code is used for men's cotton shirts?

We invoice men's woven cotton shirts under HSN 62052090 and hand woven cotton shirts under 62052010. Your import tariff code is confirmed by your customs broker in your country.

What GSM should I choose for a cotton T shirt?

Most retail T shirts sit between 160 and 200 GSM. Below 150 GSM feels light and summery; 200 GSM and above reads as heavyweight or premium. We can send fabric swatches at two or three weights before you decide.

Can you produce jeans to my wash standard?

Yes. Send a physical wash standard or a reference pair and we match it on the sample. Approved wash standards are kept for bulk comparison.

Do you make fully lined blazers?

Yes. Blazers can be unlined, half lined or fully lined. Tell us the lining fabric or send a reference and we include it in the costing.
