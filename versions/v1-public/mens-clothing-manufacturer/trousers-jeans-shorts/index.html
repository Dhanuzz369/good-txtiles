About this range

Men's trousers, jeans and shorts are classified together under HSN 6203 by fibre. Cotton trousers, jeans and shorts share 62034290 on our invoices; synthetic fibre trousers take 62034300.

Fit is what makes or breaks a bottoms programme. Send a fit sample or your measurement chart with waist, inseam, rise, thigh and leg opening, and we grade it across your size range before cutting.

Specification options

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

Can you produce jeans to my wash standard?

Yes. Send a physical wash standard or a reference pair and we match it on the sample. Approved wash standards are kept for bulk comparison.

Are jeans and cotton trousers the same HSN code?

On our invoices, men's cotton trousers, jeans and shorts are all declared under 62034290, because the code follows the garment type and fibre rather than the weave.
