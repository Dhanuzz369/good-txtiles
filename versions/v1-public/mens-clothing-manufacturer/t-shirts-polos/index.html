About this range

T shirts are knitted garments under HSN chapter 61. Single jersey is the standard T shirt fabric; polo shirts are usually knitted in pique. Polyester T shirts are typically performance or sports fabrics with moisture management finishes.

GSM is the number most buyers lead with. A lighter tee feels softer and costs less; a heavier tee hangs better and reads as premium. Tell us the GSM you sell today and we match it, or we suggest one for your price point.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Anything else? Send us your question.

What GSM should I choose for a cotton T shirt?

Most retail T shirts sit between 160 and 200 GSM. Below 150 GSM feels light and summery; 200 GSM and above reads as heavyweight or premium. We can send fabric swatches at two or three weights before you decide.

Do you print or embroider logos on T shirts and polos?

Yes. Screen printing, DTF and embroidery are available. Send your artwork with Pantone references and placement measurements.
